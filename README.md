# 🚀 MAP Test Adventure

Interactive MAP Growth Test practice for **two grade levels** — aligned with both US and UK school systems.

> **Live App**: https://mrpawelcz.github.io/map-test/

---

## 🌍 US vs UK Grade Equivalence

| Age | 🇺🇸 US Grade | 🇬🇧 UK Year | MAP Test |
|-----|-------------|------------|---------|
| 9–10 | **Grade 4** | **Year 5** | MAP Growth 2–5 |
| 10–11 | **Grade 5** | **Year 6** | MAP Growth 2–5 / 6+ |

---

## 📚 Two Levels

### Grade 4 / Year 5 (age 9–10)
- **Math**: Place value, rounding, multi-digit operations, fractions (equivalent, compare, add/subtract), mixed numbers, decimal fractions, patterns, factors/multiples, area, perimeter, geometry, symmetry
- **Reading**: Vocabulary, 3 informational passages (bees, Arctic fox, water cycle), figurative language (metaphor, simile, personification, hyperbole, idiom, alliteration, onomatopoeia), literary elements, text features
- **Language**: Capitalization, punctuation, parts of speech, subject-verb agreement, pronouns, verb tenses, complete sentences, spelling, homophones

### Grade 5 / Year 6 (age 10–11)
- **Math**: Multi-digit multiplication & long division, decimal operations (to thousandths), fraction multiplication & division, order of operations (PEMDAS), volume, coordinate planes, algebraic expressions & equations, powers of 10, unit conversion, GCF/LCM, mean/median/mode/range
- **Reading**: Complex passages (biography, science, history), comparing texts, author's argument & evidence, word roots (Greek/Latin), narrator reliability, textual evidence
- **Language**: Verb moods, participial phrases, advanced comma rules, colons & semicolons, commonly confused words (affect/effect, etc.), active vs passive voice, sentence combining

## ✨ Features

- 🧠 **Adaptive difficulty** — just like the real MAP test
- 💡 **Detailed explanations** for every answer (correct AND wrong)
- 🔀 **Shuffled answer options** — no pattern guessing
- 🌱 **Practice Mode** (no timer) + ⏱️ **Test Mode** (timed)
- 🎉 Confetti, streak counter, star ratings
- 📊 Performance breakdown by subject
- 👀 Review all mistakes with full explanations
- 📱 Works on tablets and computers

---

## 🛠️ Tech

Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript — single file per level, no dependencies, works offline.

*Based on NWEA MAP Growth standards. Grade 4 = UK Year 5, Grade 5 = UK Year 6.*
